About

Every womanstarts somewhere

I’ve had the privilege of coaching more than 600 women, with different goals and different bodies, and watching them achieve results they didn’t believe they had in them.

What I learned

What actuallyworked

The opposite of what most women are told. You lift properly. You eat enough, especially protein. If you want to lose fat, we set a real deficit you can actually live on, not the punishing one you have been trying and failing at for years. No crash diets, no fads, no quick fixes. Just genuine accountability and consistency.

And then a few weeks in, you lift something you’d have called impossible, and something shifts. You stop asking what your body looks like and start asking what it can do. The fat loss, when that’s what you wanted, comes as a side effect of finally doing it in a way you can sustain.

That’s why this app exists. To reach as many women as we can, and give them what we found: the results, and the way it feels to finally stop fighting your own body.

Because it was never only about how you look. It’s about how you feel.

Where this comes from

Why I careabout this so much

I should tell you where the anger comes from, because it shapes everything in the app.

I was sixteen when fitness culture nearly took me apart. I got out. It took years, it took help, and it was nothing like a straight line. What I came out of it with was a very specific intolerance for how much of what gets sold to women as health isn’t health at all.

That’s not the whole of me and it’s not the whole of this app. Most of the women I’ve trained have never had an eating disorder. They just wanted to lose weight, or get strong, or be able to carry their kid up the stairs without stopping. All of that is welcome here, and all of it is treated with the same respect.

It just means that when I built this, I knew exactly which shortcuts not to take.

Why the app is like this

Built from realcoaching, not theory

Every decision in ALTER YOU comes from years of coaching real women in person, and seeing what actually works and what quietly holds them back.

  1. 01

    It will never promise a number by a date

    Losing fat is a completely legitimate goal, and ALTER YOU is built to get you there. What it won’t do is tell you it takes four weeks. Every program says how long it really takes, before you start it.

  2. 02

    The scale is never the only headline

    Not because your weight doesn’t matter to you. Because if you lift while you lose fat, the number on the scale is the least informative thing in the room, and treating it as the score has broken more women than it has helped.

  3. 03

    There’s a Big Mac in the food library

    No food is banned. Not one. A plan you have to break isn’t a plan, and the moment an app tells a woman a food is forbidden, it has started teaching her the thing I spent years unlearning.

  4. 04

    Your calories can’t be set dangerously low

    There’s a hard floor in the code. You can’t get under it, no matter what you type, and no matter how much you want to. This is the single decision I’m most certain about.

  5. 05

    It tells you to go heavier

    Most women train for a year and lift the same weight the whole time, because nothing ever tells them to add to the bar. ALTER YOU does, the week you have earned it, and it won’t flatter you in the weeks you haven’t.

  6. 06

    It will never panic you

    No streak about to die. No red badge. No notification at 9pm telling you that you have failed. You’re an adult with a life, and the app is a tool, not a supervisor.

The founder

MeetRoxy

For years my relationship with my body was a war.

An eating disorder, a head full of negative self-talk, endless cardio, overtraining and under-eating, and a cycle that left me insecure and exhausted every single day. I remember crying and wondering if any of it was worth it.

Weight training is what turned it around. Fuelling my body instead of punishing it, training to be strong instead of small. Somewhere along the way I fell in love with feeling strong, and I still think it’s the best feeling in the world.

It made me the happiest and healthiest I’ve ever been, and it became the whole reason I started coaching women, and later the reason I built ALTER YOU.

I’ve coached more than 600 women in person. I built ALTER YOU to be the app I needed when I was at my lowest.

If you’re where I was

ALTER YOU is a fitness app. It’s not treatment and it’s not a substitute for treatment. If your relationship with food or your body is hurting you right now, a training app isn’t what you need first, and I’d rather say that plainly than take your subscription.

Reaching out for real support was the single best thing I ever did, and it’s the only part of my story I’d tell you to copy. A qualified professional can help in a way an app never can. In Australia, the Butterfly Foundation National Helpline is on 1800 33 4673.

What you’re paying for

Nobody hereis paid to keep you

There’s nobody whose job it’s to invent a reason to keep you subscribed. No ads. No selling your data. No dark pattern that hides the cancel button three menus deep. You cancel in two taps, from inside the app, and I won’t make it harder than that.

It also means your feedback lands with the person who can actually act on it. Email me. I read all of it, and I’ve been on the other side of most of it.
