Behind the app

Built by women,for women

ALTER YOU was founded and built by Roxanne Alterio, a Brisbane personal trainer who has coached over 600 women in person. Every program, every principle and every decision in this app came from her, drawn from years on the gym floor rather than from a template.

Training women face to face is where the app comes from: real women at every stage there is, most of them arriving convinced their body was a problem to be solved, many already hurt by form nobody had shown them. That’s why ALTER YOU has a form library on every single exercise.

I came through my own eating disorder. I know what it costs to spend years at war with your own body, and I’m not building an app that starts that fight in anybody else.

That’s the whole reason ALTER YOU behaves the way it does. Strength through every season a woman goes through, not in spite of it.

Before you sign up

ALTER YOU isn’tfor everyone

Right for you if

  • You want to lose fat and keep it off
  • You want to build strength, not simply become smaller
  • You have trained consistently and seen little change
  • You can give it two sessions a week, not five

Not right for you if

  • You want rapid weight loss. Fat loss is fully supported here, but it’s measured in months.
  • You want a meal plan that bans entire food groups.
  • You want a program you can finish. This one doesn’t end.
  • You’re struggling with food or your body right now. That deserves proper support, not an app. Please read this first.
