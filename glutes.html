Why it works

Load, thenadd to it

Glutes grow the same way every other muscle does: put them under real load, then make that load heavier over time. Most women train for a year and lift the same weight the whole way through, because nothing ever tells them to go up. This program does, the week your logged sets say you have earned it.

The four lifts

What you’llbe doing

  1. 01

    Hip thrust

    The one that loads the glutes hardest at the top. Chin tucked, ribs down, and drive through the heel rather than arching your back to finish the rep.

  2. 02

    Romanian deadlift

    Hinge, don’t squat. You should feel it in the hamstrings on the way down. If your lower back is doing the work, the bar has drifted away from your legs.

  3. 03

    Bulgarian split squat

    The one everyone avoids, and the one that fixes side to side differences. Lean forward slightly to bias the glute over the quad.

  4. 04

    Cable kickback

    Small, controlled, and not a leg swing. Squeeze at the top and let it come back slowly. This is where the last bit of shape comes from.
