ALTER YOU — website files
=========================

Everything in THIS folder goes onto your web host, keeping the
structure exactly as it is here.

  /                     <- all the .html, .css, .js, .xml, .txt files
  /results/             <- the 7 transformation photos, in their own folder

That is the whole job. Do not rename anything, and do not move the
results folder.

THE TWO FILES THAT BREAK EVERYTHING IF MISSED
---------------------------------------------
  alter.css   every page loads it. Without it the site is plain text.
  alter.js    every page loads it. Without it the menu, carousels and
              photos stop working.

If the site looks unstyled after uploading, one of those two did not
make it, or landed in a subfolder instead of the root.

TWO FILES NAMED index-ish
-------------------------
  index.html   your HOMEPAGE   (hero reads "Bikini Build")
  admin.html   your ADMIN      (title reads "ALTER Admin")
Both go in the root. They are different files with different names,
so nothing can overwrite anything.

AFTER UPLOADING
---------------
Open  alteryouapp.com/check.html  in a browser. It tests the CSS, the
JavaScript, the fonts and the database connection, and names the fix
for anything that fails. Delete check.html once you are happy.

Then see DO-NOT-UPLOAD/READ-ME-FIRST.md for the database step and the
pre-launch checklist.
