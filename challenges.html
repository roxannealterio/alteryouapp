How it runs

Four steps,every round

  1. 01

    Register in the app

    Tap join before the start date and lock your spot. Everyone begins together on day one.

  2. 02

    Get your plan

    Training and macros built around your answers. You’re never left guessing what to do.

  3. 03

    Train with the cohort

    Log every session, chase your numbers, and lean on a whole community pushing through the same weeks.

  4. 04

    Finish and compare

    Look at the data: the sessions you did and the weight you added. The biggest transformation, by work done, takes the prize.

The prize

Judged on the work,never on a body

Every round has a winner, and it’s never "who looks best". The prize goes to the biggest transformation measured the only way that’s fair: the strength you built and the sessions you showed up for. Progress you earned, not a number on a scale.
