What it’s

Winter isfor building

You can’t lean out something you haven’t built yet. Bikini Build is the part almost everyone skips: eight weeks of eating enough and adding weight to the bar, so that when spring comes there’s a shape underneath to reveal.

The training

Glutes, legsand a stronger back

Lower body led, with real upper body work so you finish balanced rather than bottom heavy. Four days if you have them, three if you don’t, and a two day version that’s a genuine program rather than a consolation prize.

  • Hip thrusts, RDLs and split squats as the backbone
  • Tempo and target effort on every single set
  • A form guide on every lift, with the mistake most people make
  • The app tells you the week you have earned more weight

The food

Eating enoughis the point

You can’t build on a deficit. Your calories and protein are worked out from your own body, with a hard floor underneath that you can’t set below. There are 101 recipes to hit the number with, and nothing is off the table.

  • Targets from your height, weight, age and how much you move
  • Log in grams, or scan a barcode
  • Save recipes and the grocery list builds itself

How it runs

Four steps,eight weeks

  1. 01

    Join before it starts

    Tap join in the app and your spot is locked. Everyone begins on the same day, which is most of the reason it works.

  2. 02

    Get your build program

    Training and macros built around your answers: your days, your equipment, your experience. You’re never left guessing what to do.

  3. 03

    Add weight, week over week

    Log every set. When your logged reps say you have earned it, the app tells you to go heavier, and by how much.

  4. 04

    Finish and compare

    At the end you can see the sessions you did and the weight you added. The biggest transformation, by work done, takes the prize.

The prize

Judged on the work,never on a body

The winner takes a full year of ALTER YOU. It goes to the biggest transformation, measured the only way that’s fair: the sessions you completed, the weight you added, and how consistently you stuck to your own program. Never on how you look, and never by a random draw.

Full challenge and prize terms are in our Terms of Service, clause 9.

Before you join

Bikini Build isn’tfor everyone

Right for you if

  • You want to build shape, not just get smaller
  • You have trained a while and stayed at the same weights
  • You can give it two sessions a week or more
  • You’re willing to eat enough for eight weeks

Not right for you if

  • You want to lose weight fast. This is a build, and the scale may go up.
  • You want to finish in four weeks. Eight is the minimum for the numbers to move.
  • You’re struggling with food or your body right now. That deserves real support. Please read this first.
