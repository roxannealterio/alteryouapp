ALTER YOU — website
===================

WHAT TO DO
----------
1. Unzip this file.
2. Upload EVERYTHING from the unzipped folder into your website's root
   (the folder your host serves alteryouapp.com from, often called
   public_html, www or htdocs).
3. Keep the "results" folder as a folder. Do not rename anything.
4. Open  alteryouapp.com/check.html  and read what it says.
5. In Supabase, open the SQL editor, paste in _setup/SITE_IMAGES.sql,
   and run it once. That is what makes the photo slots appear in the
   admin.

That is the whole job.

WHAT IS WHERE
-------------
index.html      your homepage
admin.html      your admin  (alteryouapp.com/admin.html)
check.html      diagnostics. Open it once, then delete it.
alter.css       styling for every page
alter.js        behaviour for every page
results/        the 7 transformation photos
_setup/         these notes and the database file. Harmless if it
                gets uploaded, and you can delete it afterwards.

IF THE SITE LOOKS LIKE PLAIN TEXT
---------------------------------
alter.css or alter.js did not make it into the root. Every page loads
both of them. That is nearly always the cause.

BEFORE YOU SHARE THE SITE ANYWHERE
----------------------------------
Upload og-image.jpg in the admin, under Website Images, then Brand,
then "Share image". Until you do, posting your link to Instagram or a
group chat shows no picture.
